## I am an evolutionary biologist interested in understanding how genomic variation shapes plant evolution and adaptation. I have long been fascinated by the diversity of the plant world. For most of us, the initial appreciation of plants comes from the stunning beauty they bring to the world around us. My research interests stem from this appreciation and the curiosity to understand the remarkable diversity of the plant world, the processes that have created it, and the ways in which they adapt to an ever- changing world. Through the lens of evolutionary biology, I seek to gain a better understanding of these patterns and processes. I am fascinated by these mechanisms of plant adaptation, from changes in a single nucleotide to the addition of entire sets of chromosomes through polyploidy and, importantly, how these genetic changes shape plants’ ability to overcome the diverse challenges imposed by their environments. Ultimately, I hope to use this understanding to contribute to the conservation of the remarkable plant diversity around us.
